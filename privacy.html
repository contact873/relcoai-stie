Privacy notice.

What we collect when you contact us, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we receive the details you type in: your name, email address, message and any optional fields you fill in (company, phone, website, service of interest, budget). We also receive the page address you sent the form from.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote or audit, and run the work if you decide to proceed. We do not sell your details and we do not add you to marketing lists without asking.

How it is handled

Your message is sent by email to our inbox at contact@raschident.com through a transactional email provider. The form is hosted on Vercel, which processes the request. We keep correspondence for as long as it is useful for the relationship and legal record-keeping, and you can ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing contact@raschident.com.

This is a plain-language summary. It is not legal advice and may be replaced with a formal policy.
