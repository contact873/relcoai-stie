An engine, not paintwork.

Connect is the growth practice of Relco Digital. We build the systems behind growth and stay to keep them working.

Why Connect exists.

Most businesses do not have a marketing problem. They have a system that never got built: leads that arrive by chance, work done by hand, and no one accountable for the result.

Connect fixes the engine rather than the paintwork. We combine strategy, web development, automation and marketing in one team so that the plan, the build and the measurement are done by people who talk to each other.

We work remotely and worldwide. Clients have included manufacturers in the UAE, software companies in Germany, e-commerce brands in the UK and professional services firms across Asia.

The founder.

Misbah ul Alam is the founder and growth strategist. He writes every audit himself, and every plan is built around the client's own numbers.
